Grammar

Relative Pronouns

Relative Pronouns
Relative Pronoun Use Example
that / which objects The computer that I use to work is brand new.
This pen, which is extremely cheap, is really good.
where places The place where John lives is called Bergen.
when moments (time) The day when the crime was committed was cloudy.
why reason The reason why John lives in Canada is because he loves it.
who people (direct) John, who is my neighbour, always wakes up early.
whom people (indirect) The friend whom I called yesterday was not at home.
whose possession May, whose sister is a musician, plays the piano really well.
how the way we do things Practicing a lot is how I finally got my driving license.
