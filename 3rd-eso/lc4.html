Grammar

Past Simple & Past Continuous

Main Structure
Past Continuous + when + Past Simple I was driving when my phone rang.
Past Simple + while + Past Continuous I fell while I was dancing.
Inversions & Variations
When + Past Simple, Past Continuous When my phone rang, I was driving.
While + Past Continuous, Past Simple While I was dancing, I fell.
While + Past Continuous, Past Continuous While I was studying, my sister was watching TV.

Too / Too much / Too many / (not) Enough

Form, Use & Examples
Form Use Example
too + adj More than wanted (degree) It's too cold outside.
too much + noun More than wanted (uncountable) She eats too much sugar.
too many + noun More than wanted (countable) There are too many people.
enough + noun The right amount We have enough time.
not enough + noun Less than needed There isn't enough bread.
adj + enough Sufficient degree She's old enough to vote.
not + adj + enough Insufficient degree I am not tall enough to be a model.
