2nd ESO – LC0

Grammar

Present Simple (To Be)

I → am

You, we, they → are

He, she, it → is

Present Simple (Actions)

I, we, you, they → play / don't play

He, she, it → plays / doesn't play
Do + I, we, you, they + play?

Does + he, she, it + play?

Keywords

Present Continuous

I → am playing / am not playing

You, we, they → are playing / aren't playing

He, she, it → is playing / isn't playing
Am + I + playing?

Are + you, we, they + playing?

Is + he, she, it + playing?
