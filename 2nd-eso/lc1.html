Grammar

Past Simple (To Be)

I, he, she, it → was / wasn't / Was … ?

We, you, they → were / weren't / Were … ?

Past Simple (Regular)

Regular verbs

+ Subject + verb + ed
− Subject + didn't + infinitive
? Did + subject + infinitive?

Past Simple (Irregular)

Irregular verbs

+ Subject + 2nd column of the verbs list
− Subject + didn't + infinitive
? Did + subject + infinitive?

There was / There were

There was → singular (countable: a tree) & uncountable (money, water…)
There were → plural countable (two trees)

Informal Email: 'A Fun Day Out'

Informal Email: 'A Fun Day Out'
Structure Example
Greeting Hi Jane!
Opening How are you? I hope you're well. I want to tell you about last Saturday… it was amazing! It was one of the best days I can remember.
Body (paragraph 1 + paragraph 2 + question) Last Saturday, I went on a trip with my family to a beautiful village in Mallorca called Valldemossa. The village is in the mountains and it's very quiet and beautiful. The houses are old and the streets are small and nice.

We walked around the village, visited a museum and ate delicious Mallorcan food. I took lots of photos! The weather was sunny and warm, so it was perfect.

I really loved the trip. Have you ever been to Valldemossa?
Closing Write soon!
Goodbye Best wishes,
Signature Sofía

Project Builder 3

Write your script (what you are going to say) and create your presentation.

How to prepare your presentation?

⚠️ IMPORTANT: you can't include text (paragraphs) or long sentences in your presentation, only keywords. You can use images and other visual resources.

  • 1 slide to introduce yourselves (name of the group, names of the members, time era)
  • 1 slide (at least) for each category: Time period and country · Clothes · Food · Entertainment · Technology
  • 1 slide with your objects for the Time Capsule
  • 1 slide to finish the presentation ('Thank you for your attention', 'thank you for listening', etc.)

⏳ Each member is going to speak for, at least, 1 minute (apart from the intro and outro).

Final Project
